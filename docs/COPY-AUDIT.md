# Copy audit — costume language out

Occasion-grounded editorial. Age is a legal line. It is not a look.

| Where | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fashion-clock` week note | prep | navy and white |
| `fashionPulse.prompt` | Member is 18–24 (Gen Z). Do not lock this person into a generation costume. | Member is 18–24. Dress the day they named. |
| `style-me` alpha voice | Do not invent a 'tween aesthetic' | School or play. Do not invent a generation costume. |
| `style-me` teen voice | Do not invent a 'prep' or 'Y2K' script | School, home, family. Do not invent a generation costume. |
| Welcome kicker | Confirm generation | How old today |
| Welcome dek | Gen Z · 24 today | 24 today · season |
| Welcome cards | years + Gen Alpha / Young Z / … | years only |
| Shell toast | Your generation updated · Gen Z | The rail follows you now · 18–24 |
| Shell birthday | Your generation — and the rail — updated | Birthday noted. The rail updated. |

## Left as identifiers, not style

- `GENERATIONS[].gen` remains in data (`Gen Alpha` … `Boomer+`) for tests and research. It is not painted as a look.
- Code field `vibe` stores the **occasion label** (Work, Gym, Date). The member never sees the word "vibe".
- Practice essay names "quiet luxury / modern uniform / heritage" only to refuse them.

## Ban list (must not appear in member-facing copy)

`prep`, `Y2K`, `twin houses`, `core`, `aesthetic`, `vibe` as a style word, era labels, generation nicknames as a look.
